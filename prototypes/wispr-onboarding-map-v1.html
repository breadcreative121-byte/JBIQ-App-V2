JBIQ · AI Saathi · Onboarding teardown

Wispr Flow’s onboarding, mapped — and cut for Bharat

The full first-run flow pulled screen-by-screen from Mobbin, grouped into the six jobs it does. Under every real screen is the JBIQ Saathi translation — what we keep, and the two things we deliberately cut. The prize is the highlighted phase: the user speaks once and sees magic, in a single turn.

The spine: Frame the value & pain → minimal-feeling account → explain-then-permit the mic → speak once & see magic → extend to real use → reward + teach the hard gesture last.

Two cuts for JBIQ: (1) no account or email-verify before the win — the first task runs anonymously; (2) the first utterance is a real Jio task (a paid bill, a live darshan), not a throwaway “say anything” demo.

The adaptation

The JBIQ Saathi cut — five screens to a first win

Wispr earns the win at screen 6–8 of ~12, behind a full account. Strip the account and make the first utterance real, and Saathi reaches the same aha in five — no login, no verify, a genuine outcome.
