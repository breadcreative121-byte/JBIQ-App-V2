Moment-led home — how should we show 1 vs many live moments?

Home’s front door surfaces one live moment in the hero slot — a darshan, a match, a festival, a morning brief — and its action deep-links into the matching Space. But on a big day (say Diwali) several moments are live at once. These four representations show how the state behaves with one moment and with four.

The tension: the front-door principle is “one live moment, never a feed.” The question is what happens to the other three when they’re all live — do they vanish, get acknowledged, or compete?

Control · current

1 · Single hero moment

What the prototype ships today: one moment wins the hero slot, rendered rich (festive kicker, LIVE badge, deep-link CTA). With four live, priority picks one — the rest aren’t shown.

Pattern: F1 “Live coverage” hero · Fanatics Live watch-now hero.
Trade-off: maximum focus and drama for the winning moment — but on a busy day the user never learns the match or rashifal is also live. Zero sense of “what else is on right now”.

2 · Hero + “also happening now”

Keep one hero moment, but add a compact row of pills for the others. Tap a pill to promote it into the hero. One focus, but nothing is hidden — and switching is one tap.

Pattern: F1 hero + circular live rail · DAZN “Live & Coming Up”. F1
Trade-off: preserves the single-hero focus (voice-first friendly) while acknowledging the rest. Risk: the pill row can be skimmed past — mitigated by the live dots drawing the eye.

Alternative

3 · “Happening now” rail

Treat live moments as peers in a labelled, swipeable rail — each a mini card with its badge and CTA. Best when two moments are equally urgent and no single one should dominate.

Pattern: DAZN / NBA live rails · Fixtured “Happening now”. DAZN · Fixtured
Trade-off: fair to all live moments and scannable — but loses the single-hero drama and edges toward a feed, the exact thing the front door avoids. Swipe is also less discoverable for NBU.

4 live moments
Alternative

4 · Live-count strip + hero

A thin “● N happening now” strip sits above the hero and expands to the full list. Keeps a single hero moment while signalling how much else is live right now.

Pattern: Riot “Live” tab + badge · Apple Sports / NFL live scorecards. Riot · Apple Sports
Trade-off: strongest “how much is live” signal while keeping a hero — but adds chrome above the moment, and a bare count can read as busywork rather than a warm nudge.
