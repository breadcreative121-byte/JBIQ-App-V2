Components across domains

Every visual primitive shown across two or three different verticals. Same component, different content. Pick a component to see how it adapts.

Title + subtitle · optional partner row above
SubjectHeader

The frame's title block. Optional partner attribution row (logo chip + partner name) sits above the title — used wherever a single partner owns the answer (Swiggy food, VRL bus seats, Zomato deals). Brand chip swatches are part of the canonical library.
Place | Item | Person | Media (single) · or Collection in carousel
EntityCard

Media-led card. No chrome on the card itself — only the media (16:9, rounded-lg) is rounded. Same primitive renders one thing in focus or a horizontal scroll of three-to-five things to browse. Switch by cardinality, not by component.
Collection<*>, count 2–4
CompareTable

2–4 options across shared axes. Sticky 76px label column on the left, scroll-snapped option columns. Recommended option tinted; best values picked out in primary.
Collection<*> · flat or sectioned · list | grid | carousel
List

CatalogResultCard in list layout — square 80px media on the left, content on the right. Flat or sectioned. Same primitive renders a restaurant list, a restaurant's menu by category, a doctor list, job postings, news headlines.
Collection
Timeline

Vertical agenda. Time on the left, title + meta on the right. Active row highlighted with primary-surface. Same shape for table reservations, doctor slots, fixtures, calendar events.
Status | Status
Tracker

Primary-coloured tile sized like place-card media. Used for food delivery, package shipping, cricket innings, application processing — anything in motion.
Place | Collection | Route
Map

Schematic plot — single pin, multiple pins, or a route. Pairs with List in split view, insets into Tracker when Status carries Route data.
Transaction.building
CartPanel

Hairline-divided line items, totals block in secondary-surface, full-width primary CTA. Same shape for a restaurant order, a grocery basket, a service booking.
Choice (system-injected)
ConfirmPanel

The mandatory interrupt. Same secondary-surface card whether confirming a food order, a UPI transfer, a flight booking, or a medical appointment. Partners cannot bypass.
Transaction.confirmed
ReceiptPanel

The universal proof. Tick + title at the top, hairline-divided line items, totals row in bold. Same primitive for a food order, UPI transfer, appointment, Aadhaar SRN.
Choice
ChoiceChips

Pill chips with hairline borders, purple fill when selected. Same primitive for cuisine filter, zodiac, seat preference, or government sub-service.
Media (single, now playing)
MediaPlayer

Compact now-playing strip. 80px artwork, title and subtitle, hairline scrubber, transport row. Same primitive for a song, chant, podcast.
Collection
Gallery

CatalogResultCard at 3:4 media in a 2-column grid. Same primitive for menu items, movie posters, devotional collections, podcast covers.
