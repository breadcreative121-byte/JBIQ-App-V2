A map of where a business and a user sees brand presence.

The user needs trust signals. The business needs attribution. The system needs cohesion. Three things to balance, eight surfaces to balance them across.

04 Voice attribution

The invisible channel where brand only reaches the user as sound.

Voice is brand's hardest surface you can't show a logo. Attribution happens through phrasing, persona, and consistent naming.

  • Spoken partner mention "From Practo:", "On Swiggy:"
  • Persona inheritance partner's selected voice persona reflects their brand tone
  • Confirm readbacks partner always named before money moves (non-negotiable)
  • Tracker updates "Swiggy says your order's 12 minutes away"
  • Receipt acks "Your Practo appointment is confirmed"

The experience partnership

Two forces, one surface.

JBIQ cohesion

Every experience feels like one product. Predictable rhythm. Consistent voice. One safety layer.

vs

Partner attribution

Their brand seen. Their relationship preserved. Their data, their trust, their continued mindshare.

The component system has to let both win through a system of rules.

The rules

A ladder, not a switch.

Brand placement isn't on/off it's tiered. Five levels, with each placement assigned a tier on the manifest.

Required
Verification badges, confirm readbacks legal & safety. Partners can't opt out.
Default-on
Partner mark in the card title row, source line in receipts. Always there unless explicitly hidden.
Opt-in
Watermark on media, hero colour wash. The partner decides whether to show them.
Capped
Max one partner logo per card. Per-row attribution only when rows come from different sources.
Forbidden
No partner brand on system-owned chrome sidebar, safety confirm step, voice meter.
